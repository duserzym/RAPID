Outputs

Checking the 5 V

probes
Black on any GND pin of the POWER header, red on the pin under test. Not pin‑to‑pin.
HIGH
4.8 – 5.1 V on USB power. Anything under 4.5 V means the pin is loaded or the rail is sagging.
LOW
< 0.1 V. A few hundred millivolts means something is still driving the line.
D13
Also drives the onboard LED, so you get visual confirmation alongside the reading.
limit
20 mA per pin as a design figure, 40 mA absolute. Enough for a TTL input or an opto LED, not a relay coil.
